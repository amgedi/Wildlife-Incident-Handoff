## First milestone

The first validation milestone is complete when:

- five external testers have tried the RC
- at least four can complete the core incident flow without live guidance
- installation blockers are understood
- serious crashes or data-loss bugs are either fixed or documented as release blockers
- recurring confusion is converted into concrete issues

Do not record private contact details, real incident records, or sensitive wildlife locations here.
