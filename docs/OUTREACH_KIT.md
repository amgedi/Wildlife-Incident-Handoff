# Outreach kit

Wildlife Incident Handoff is looking for external RC testers. This page contains short copy you can adapt for direct messages, community posts, LinkedIn, or outreach to wildlife organizations.

## One-sentence description

Wildlife Incident Handoff is an open-source Windows desktop app for recording wildlife incidents and preserving context as information moves between reporters, responders, transporters, rehabilitation organizations, veterinary teams, conservation staff, and other people involved in a handoff.

## Short tester invite

I’ve released an external testing build and I’m looking for a few people willing to try it using fictional data and tell me what feels confusing, missing, or awkward.

Hi! I’m developing Wildlife Incident Handoff, an open-source desktop application focused on preserving information through wildlife incident reporting, response, transport, and handoff workflows.

The current build is an RC for external testing. I’m especially interested in feedback from people familiar with wildlife rehabilitation, rescue, conservation, animal control, transport, dispatch, or veterinary workflows.

## What to ask after someone agrees

Send them only what they need:

1. the RC release link
2. the 15-minute tester quickstart
3. the known limitations page
4. the tester feedback form

Avoid sending the full development documentation unless they ask for it.

## First validation target

Aim for five external testers:

- two people with no prior knowledge of the app
- two people with wildlife-adjacent experience
- one technical tester willing to stress installation, persistence, export, backup, resizing, and update behavior

The useful milestone is not a particular star count. It is five independent people completing the core flow and showing where the product needs work.

## Pilot conversation

After the RC has been tested successfully by several people, approach organizations with a smaller ask than adoption:

> Would one or two members of your team be willing to test a fictional incident workflow and tell me where it differs from your real process?

That creates a path toward a real pilot without asking an organization to commit to unproven software.
